## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the ambition, precision, and architectural gravity of India's population-scale sovereign AI platform. It merges institutional authority with state-of-the-art computational velocity. The design language operates at the intersection of deep-space compute infrastructure and cultural resonance—anchored by an obsidian-grade midnight canvas, energized by radiant saffron/amber solar energy, and sharpened by electric indigo/cyan spectral diagnostics.

The target audience encompasses enterprise architects, platform developers, national data stewards, and enterprise executives who demand zero-trust sovereign compliance alongside world-class model throughput. The UI evokes absolute reliability, national pride without ornament, developer clarity, and unyielding computational power.

The visual style blends **Neo-Corporate Technical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal foundation layers provide zero-glare, high-contrast operational efficiency.
- Hairline glassmorphic panels and micro-chamfered cards mimic high-density server blades and cleanroom hardware.
- Saffron and amber accents act as radiant energy conductors, symbolizing sovereign warmth and sovereign pride, while indigo and cyan deliver sharp algorithmic feedback.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-performance dark environment engineered for visual comfort during extended engineering sessions while commanding respect at the executive level:

- **Primary (`#FF9933`)**: Radiant Saffron represents the core pulse of sovereign compute. Used deliberately for focal interactive elements, hero call-to-actions, active telemetry markers, and high-impact indicators.
- **Secondary (`#6366F1`)**: Deep Electric Indigo balances saffron warmth with cold structural logic. Applied to developer switches, active API methods, model weight indicators, and secondary pipelines.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4`)**: Diagnostic Cyan delivers crisp real-time telemetry, model latency callouts, and networking status metrics.
- **Neutral (`#0A0D14`)**: A black-blue space canvas that preserves contrast and depth without muddy grayness.

### Application Guidelines
- **Color Discipline**: Saffron is never used for background fills greater than button scale. It must remain a beacon of intent.
- **Radial Ambiance**: Dark canvases are enriched with low-opacity (4%–12%) radial atmospheric gradients blending saffron into indigo to suggest active sovereign silicon.
- **State Semantics**: Success triggers use `#10B981`, and failures/declines deploy `#EF4444`. Muted data labels utilize de-saturated cool whites (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an unwavering cadence between executive authority and developer tooling:

- **Primary Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers an athletic, razor-sharp geometric balance. Its tight tracking and high x-height anchor display titles, step headings, and documentation copy without sacrificing human approachability.
- **Precision Engine (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Powers numeric step indexes (`01`, `02`), API code snippets, Indic language tags (`hi-IN`, `bulbul:v3`), latency badges (`<100ms`), and tabular telemetry.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Eyebrows & Sovereign Tags**: Rendered in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` or uppercase `JetBrains Mono` with loose tracking (`0.08em`) to delineate operational domain (e.g., `SOVEREIGN INFERENCE`, `INDIC DIARIZATION`).
- **Tabular Alignment**: All numbers across analytics cards, funnel metrics, and benchmark logs must enforce tabular figure alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Clarity Standards**: Code snippets maintain rigid syntax hierarchies with cyan for declarations, saffron for methods, and muted indigo for arguments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an uncompromising 12-column fluid grid calibrated for high-density compute monitors, standard laptops, and executive mobile briefings.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width 1360px centered, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid, dynamic viewport stretch, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single-track flow, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Data Densities**: Enterprise tables and code inspector panels use dense vertical spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize visible information per fold.
- **Narrative Sections**: Feature showcases and hero blocks leverage `space-xl` and section offsets (up to `5rem` desktop) to create monumental scale.
- **Step Process Showcase**: Split 5-to-7 column composition on desktop pairing indexed narrative steps (`01` through `05`) on the left with live compute panels on the right.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is communicated not through traditional drop shadows, but through **tonal stratification, frosted glass backdrops, and atmospheric luminosity**:

- **Ground Zero (Canvas)**: `#0A0D14` serves as the sovereign compute foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Section Containers)**: `#0F1420` framed with 1px hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Modules & Spreadsheets)**: `#151B2B` with micro-frosted backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Context Panels & Dropdowns)**: `#1E2638` with subtle saffron-tinted ambient glows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.2)`).

### Atmospheric Glow Mechanics
- Key interactive boundaries employ edge-lit gradients: a linear gradient border moving subtly from `rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.4)` to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.1)` to transparent.
- Deep background focal spots feature stationary radial glows: `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% -20%, rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.08), rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.04) 60%, transparent 80%)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural discipline through **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, reflecting the engineered precision of server modules and high-precision instruments:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Code blocks, inline metric tags, table row cells, form inputs, and tab triggers.
- **Container Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Interactive preview windows, mock spreadsheets, SDK playgrounds, and feature cards.
- **Structural Hero Containers (`0.75rem` / 12px)**: Large canvas panels and primary modal overlays.
- **Pills (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for compliance tags (`SOC 2 Type II`, `DPDP compliant`), language switchers (`Python`, `cURL`), and status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Radiant saffron fill (`#FF9933`), crisp dark-charcoal text (`#0A0D14`), semi-bold weight. Hover triggers subtle bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 153, 51, 0.45)`). Active scales down to `98%`.
- **Secondary Developer CTA**: Dark container background (`#151B2B`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), white text (`#FFFFFF`). Hover shifts border to `#6366F1` with an inner wash of indigo.
- **Icon / Ghost CTA**: Zero background, muted text (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), saffron or cyan color shift on hover with immediate feedback.

### Chips & Compliance Pills
- High-radius (`9999px`) badge geometry.
- Dark semi-translucent fill (`rgba(15, 20, 32, 0.8)`) paired with a micro-border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- Left-aligned status dot (3px pulsing green for active SLA, or solid amber for certified sovereignty standards such as DPDP).

### Data Tables & Funnel Spreadsheets
- **Headers**: Monospace micro-labels (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px uppercase) over hairline separator (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Cells**: Compact vertical padding, tabular numeric data, alternating subtle rows (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.01)`).
- **Trend Tags**: Numerical changes rendered in monospace pills (`+6.1%` in soft emerald tint, `-14.1%` in soft crimson tint).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background surface `#0F1420` with `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus state activates an electric saffron hairline ring with zero heavy offset: `outline: none; border-color: #FF9933; box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #FF9933`.
- Placeholder text: Muted cool gray (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).

### Interactive Code Modules
- Surface in deep `#0B0F17` framed by hairline dividers.
- Tab bar at top left (`Python`, `Node.js`, `cURL`) with saffron underline on active state.
- Floating `Copy` action button on top right featuring copy confirmation status change (`Copied!` in cyan).

### Step Process Cards
- Large tabular index numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`) in de-saturated indigo or saffron glow.
- Paired descriptive title and summary text that dynamically trigger active visual states on associated preview panels.
